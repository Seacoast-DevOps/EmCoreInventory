EMCore — Permissions reference

Updated November 16, 2026. This reference supersedes the earlier guide’s Service Admin and seal-witness rules. Full technical and help guide.

Role and license work together

In Users, assign one clinical license (Driver, EMT, AEMT, or Paramedic), a company role (User, Supervisor, or Company Admin), and capability tags. Seacoast Admin is a separate platform grant that only another Seacoast Admin can manage. Company policies, medication rules, Read Only, and capability tags may further restrict access; they cannot expand the matrix below.

Supervisors and both administrator roles keep their clinical restrictions when administering or wasting medication. Narcotic administration and partial waste require an actual Paramedic license. Every medication also has a minimum clinical license in Manage Par Levels. Existing definitions default to Paramedic until an authorized formulary manager reviews and changes them.

Example: an EMT who is also Company Admin can manage company contacts and formulary, but cannot administer a narcotic or a medication whose minimum license is AEMT or Paramedic.

Daily use

Storage access: Container / Storage Designer → edit a container → select its allowed roles. A transfer requires access to both the source and destination. Driver accounts cannot transfer even if given Can Transfer. Same-container moves remain blocked.

Seal actions: Drug Checks → expand container → Break seal or Reseal. Confirm the physical action and optionally record an incident number. A Driver then needs a different active user in the company, with Can Witness, to review and enter their own username and password. Cancelled or unsuccessful signoff saves no seal change.

Discrepancies: Expand a container → Report discrepancy. A Supervisor, Company Admin, or Seacoast Admin can review and approve with resolution notes on the Discrepancies page. Approval records review; it does not change vial quantities or mark missing items present.

Companies / DEA: Company Admins edit their selected company’s contact and DEA fields, including Service Chief, DEA Contact, Backup DEA Contact, Medical Director, and Assistant Medical Director. Contact titles do not grant account permissions. Seacoast Admins additionally create companies, change company permissions, deactivate/reactivate, and hard-delete companies.

Deactivation revokes company sessions and blocks routine operations while retaining data. Hard deletion requires the exact company name, the administrator’s password, and acknowledgement. It purges that company’s operational database and memberships; global user accounts and a company-deletion audit entry remain. External backups are independent and are not deleted by this action.

Technical implementation

shared/matrix-rules.js defines ceilings, clinical restrictions, and storage-role checks. shared/access-rules.js combines those rules with existing policies. The API checks authorization independently of UI controls. Client-supplied roles cannot change the signed-in identity.

server/matrix.js migrates medication classification and storage access, persists discrepancies, and records seal actions. server/company-auth.js separates global clinical license/platform role from company membership. server/company-lifecycle.js handles audited company status and deletion.

Migrations preserve vial IDs, permanent codes, stock balances, and history. Legacy Service Admin memberships become Company Admin. The original setup administrator becomes Seacoast Admin for platform recovery; later company administrators are not automatically promoted.
